## The Opportunity

Web3 and AI-agent commerce are generating more value and more disputes.

- Paying an unknown AI agent is high risk if the deliverable is incomplete or broken.
- Marketplaces, freelance platforms, NFT escrows, DAO contributor payments and agent-to-agent deals all produce multi-source evidence (contracts, chats, documents, code, on-chain transactions).
- Existing arbitration protocols (Kleros has resolved >1,500 disputes since 2018 with 760+ active jurors) solve **who wins**.
- They do not solve **what evidence is relevant, verified, contradictory and confidence-scored**, nor do they provide an automated audit layer before human judgment.

The missing layer is **evidence intelligence + smart custody**.

AgentCourt AI sits between the raw dispute and the final decision maker (juror, DAO, escrow contract or agent court). It locks funds, audits delivery, maps evidence and hands a clear package to the authorized decision maker.

---

## Product (as shown in the concept video)

1. **Smart Custody** — Funds locked in a digital vault until conditions are met.
2. **Automated Audit** — Engine reads code, conversations and deliverables.
3. **Evidence Map** — Timeline, contradictions, confidence scores, full source attribution.
4. **Human / Protocol Decision** — Decentralized jury or authorized arbitrator rules with clear evidence.
5. **Settlement** — Funds release or refund according to the ruling.

**Critical design choice:**  
AgentCourt AI **never** issues the final ruling.  
Every finding retains its source and confidence score. The authorized arbitrator remains in control. This is required for institutional and protocol adoption.

## Competitive Landscape

Several projects use the name “AgentCourt” or similar (Base escrow + multi-LLM jury, 0G TEE arbitration, academic court simulations).  

AgentCourt AI is differentiated by:

1. **Evidence-first + custody** — decision support and fund protection, not automated justice.
2. **Explicit honesty** — capability flags, no fake production features.
3. **Protocol-agnostic design** — intended to feed Kleros and others, not replace them.
4. **Human + agent dual consumption** — reports usable by both.
5. **Multi-chain architecture path** — Stellar, EVM, Solana, Monad readiness.

This is infrastructure positioning, not a competing court.
