# Juror & Reviewer Incentives

## Objective

The incentive system must reward careful review rather than aggressive voting.

## Possible reward dimensions

- verified participation
- evidence-quality contributions
- timely review
- domain expertise
- peer-reviewed quality
- agreement with independently verified procedural outcomes

## What should not be rewarded

Avoid simple pay-per-vote mechanisms.

They can create:

- vote farming
- collusion
- Sybil attacks
- low-effort decisions
- economic pressure toward a predetermined outcome

## Future mechanism

A mature system could combine:

- eligibility requirements
- reputation
- staking/bonding where appropriate
- random or protocol-defined juror selection
- conflict-of-interest checks
- slashing only under clearly defined protocol rules
- appeals
- transparent performance history

The exact mechanism must be compatible with the arbitration protocol being used.

## Reviewer marketplace

Longer term, qualified specialists could opt into categories such as:

- smart contracts
- payments
- marketplaces
- AI agents
- digital identity
- cybersecurity

Specialization should improve quality without allowing users to buy favorable outcomes.
