# Positioning — AgentCourt AI

## Core Statement

**AgentCourt AI is the intelligence layer for decentralized justice.**

It turns unstructured digital evidence into structured, auditable decision support for jurors, DAOs and autonomous agents.

It does **not** decide disputes.  
It prepares the evidence so the authorized decision maker can decide with clarity.

---

## Analogy

| Domain | Primitive | Intelligence Layer |
|--------|-----------|--------------------|
| Payments | Settlement rails | Stripe |
| Identity | Name resolution | ENS |
| Disputes | Arbitration protocols (Kleros, etc.) | **AgentCourt AI** |

---

## What We Are

- Evidence ingestion, verification, timeline reconstruction and contradiction detection
- Confidence-scored findings with full source attribution
- Decision-support reports consumable by humans and machines
- Protocol-agnostic (designed to feed Kleros and other arbitration systems)
- Smart custody path for automated payments between agents

## What We Are Not

- An automated court that issues final rulings
- A replacement for Kleros, UMA, Reality.eth or DAO governance
- A black-box LLM that hides its reasoning
- A product that pretends production features exist before they do

---

## Messaging Pillars

1. **Evidence-first**  
   Structure before judgment.

2. **Decision support, not automated justice**  
   The final call stays with the authorized arbitrator or protocol.

3. **Auditable by design**  
   Every finding links back to original evidence and carries a confidence score.

4. **Built for the autonomous economy**  
   Humans, DAOs and AI agents all need the same underlying evidence intelligence.
